About Intrix

The story behind the name.

Some people fall into technology. I was born into it.

At eight years old I had a Vic 20. At ten, a Commodore 64. Ancient history to most people, but to a kid who couldn't stop taking things apart, they were everything. By 1994 I was building my own computers from scratch. No formal training, no classroom. Just curiosity, persistence, and an obsession with figuring things out. That's still how I approach every problem today.

I spent twenty-five years in the trenches of enterprise IT, working with some of the largest financial institutions and media companies in the world before moving into pre-sales engineering at companies you'd recognize. I was good at it. But the longer I spent in that world, the harder it became to watch how people were treated.

Salespeople who vanished the moment a contract was signed. Support that stopped returning calls once the check cleared. Customers sold solutions they didn't need by people chasing a quota who'd never have to live with the consequences. No loyalty to the customer. No loyalty to the employees. Just the next deal.

I started Intrix because I wanted to do things differently. My way. Where honesty isn't a differentiator, it's the baseline. Where I won't sell you something you don't need, won't pretend to know something I don't, and won't stop until the problem is actually solved, not just closed on a ticket.

I help people because I genuinely love it. That's why Intrix serves both home users and businesses. The retired couple with a laptop problem deserves the same quality of care as a boardroom. In my world, they get it.

That's Intrix.
